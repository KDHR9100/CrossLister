# Shopee Prohibited Items & Content Rules (Summary)

> Community-maintained summary of Shopee listing and prohibited-item rules,
> used for the CrossLister RAG demo. Always verify against the latest official
> Shopee seller policies for each target market before production use.

## SPE-PROH-01: Prohibited product categories

Weapons, explosives, illegal drugs, tobacco, gambling equipment and adult
content are strictly prohibited. Listings that violate this rule are removed
and the seller account receives penalty points.

## SPE-PROH-02: Counterfeit and replica goods

Counterfeit items and unauthorized branded goods are banned, including
listings described as "1:1", "AAA" or "mirror quality". Sellers listing
branded products must hold valid authorization documents.

## SPE-PROH-03: Regulated health products

Medical devices, medicines and dietary supplements require local licenses
before listing. Claims of treating, curing or preventing diseases are
prohibited for all products.

## SPE-TITLE-01: Title format

Titles are limited to 120 characters and should follow: brand, product name,
model or specification, key attributes. Repeating keywords or stuffing
synonyms is treated as spam. Emojis and special symbols are not allowed.

## SPE-TITLE-02: Banned title words

Words like "cheapest", "lowest price" or "100% authentic" are banned unless
backed by platform certification. Only official flagship stores may use the
word "official". Competing platform names must not appear in titles.

## SPE-IMAGE-01: Image requirements

The first image must show the actual product on a clean background. Watermarks
from other platforms, QR codes, contact information and collage borders with
unrelated products are prohibited.

## SPE-CONTENT-01: Description restrictions

Descriptions must not contain external links, chat or social-media contacts,
bank account details or anything that directs buyers to transact outside the
platform.

## SPE-CONTENT-02: Misleading promotions

Fake discounts, fake countdown pressure and price-bait images are prohibited.
Displayed prices must match the actual selling price, and images must not
suggest free gifts that do not exist.

## SPE-CAT-01: Category accuracy

Products must be listed in the exact matching category. Miscategorization
leads to listing removal and reduced search ranking.

## SPE-REVIEW-01: Review manipulation

Incentivized positive reviews are prohibited, including package inserts that
promise refunds or gifts in exchange for five-star reviews.
