# Temu Content Guidelines (Summary)

> Community-maintained summary of Temu listing content rules, used for the
> CrossLister RAG demo. Always verify against the latest official Temu seller
> guidelines before production use.

## TEMU-TITLE-01: Title structure

Titles must be factual and structured as: brand (if any), product type and key
attributes such as material, color, size and pack count. Keep titles between
20 and 120 characters.

## TEMU-TITLE-02: Title restrictions

No emojis, decorative symbols or ALL-CAPS words in titles. Promotional words
such as "hot sale", "clearance", "top rated" or "free shipping" are
prohibited.

## TEMU-IMAGE-01: Main image standard

The main image must use a white background with the product centered and
occupying most of the frame. Minimum resolution is 800x800 pixels. No
watermarks, text overlays, logos or borders.

## TEMU-CONTENT-01: Absolute claims forbidden

Absolute or exaggerated language such as "best", "cheapest", "100% perfect"
or "top quality" is forbidden unless backed by authoritative certification.

## TEMU-CONTENT-02: External references forbidden

QR codes, external URLs, other marketplace names and any seller contact
information are prohibited in titles, descriptions and images.

## TEMU-CAT-01: Category and attribute accuracy

The selected category and all structured attributes must exactly match the
physical product shown in the images. Mismatches are rejected during content
review.

## TEMU-KW-01: Keyword rules

Keywords must reflect real product attributes. Keyword stuffing and irrelevant
trending words are treated as violations and reduce exposure.

## TEMU-COMPLIANCE-01: Product safety compliance

Products must satisfy destination-market safety requirements such as CE or FCC
certification where applicable. Weapons, hazardous chemicals, counterfeits and
adult content are prohibited.

## TEMU-PRICE-01: No pricing in content

Content must never mention prices, discounts or shipping costs. Pricing
display is fully controlled by the platform.

## TEMU-LANG-01: Language quality

Content must be written in the target marketplace language with accurate,
natural wording. Untranslated fragments, garbled characters or wrong size
units cause rejection.
