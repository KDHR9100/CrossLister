# Amazon Listing Policy (Summary)

> Community-maintained summary of Amazon listing rules, used for the
> CrossLister RAG demo. Always verify against the latest official Amazon
> Seller Central policies before production use.

## AMZ-TITLE-01: Maximum title length

Titles must not exceed 200 characters including spaces. Aim for roughly 80
characters so mobile search results are not truncated. Order information as:
brand, product line, material, product type, color, size and package count.

## AMZ-TITLE-02: Prohibited content in titles

Titles must not contain promotional phrases such as "sale", "best seller",
"free shipping" or "limited offer". Avoid subjective claims such as "top
quality" or "#1 choice" unless they can be substantiated. Do not use ALL-CAPS
except for brand names, and avoid decorative symbols such as !, *, $ or ?.

## AMZ-BULLET-01: Bullet point structure

Provide up to five bullet points. Keep each under 250 characters even though
500 are technically allowed. Start each bullet with the key feature followed
by the customer benefit. No HTML, URLs, pricing or seller contact details.

## AMZ-DESC-01: Product description limits

Descriptions are limited to 2000 characters. Do not include company contact
details, external website links, pricing or shipping promises. Write in
complete sentences with correct grammar and spelling.

## AMZ-KW-01: Backend search terms

Backend search terms are limited to 249 bytes. Do not repeat words already
present in the title or bullets. Never include competitor brand names, ASINs,
subjective words or abusive language. Separate terms with spaces; commas are
not required.

## AMZ-CLAIM-01: Restricted product claims

Do not make medical, therapeutic or disease-treatment claims. Claims such as
"FDA approved", "antibacterial" or "antimicrobial" require certification and
substantiation. Environmental claims like "eco-friendly" must be verifiable.

## AMZ-BRAND-01: Trademark usage

Do not use other brands' trademarks in titles, bullets or backend keywords.
Phrases like "compatible with <brand>" are only acceptable when factually true
and allowed by the category. Brand keyword misuse leads to listing suppression.

## AMZ-IMAGE-01: Main image requirements

The main image must use a pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255) with the
product filling at least 85 percent of the frame. No text, logos, watermarks,
borders or misleading props are allowed on the main image.

## AMZ-CAT-01: Category accuracy

Always select the most specific browse node that matches the product.
Miscategorization reduces search visibility and can trigger listing
suppression. Storage bins for example belong under Home & Kitchen storage and
organization.

## AMZ-VAR-01: Variation policy

Only true variations of the same product (size, color, pack count) may share
one parent listing. Adding unrelated products to inherit reviews is a policy
violation and can result in listing removal.
